How KRYPT works

Read once — it saves confusion.

✨ The basics

  • • You get a permanent code. Share it once with a friend — you never need to share it again.
  • • Add friends and chat anytime. Chats and files are saved on your phone, encrypted and locked with your PIN.
  • • 🕓 = waiting to send · ✓ = delivered · ✓✓ = seen. Press and hold a message to reply, react or delete.

📏 Limits

  • • No server holds your messages. Delivery only happens when both phones are online with KRYPT open. Messages you write while a friend is offline wait and send automatically later.
  • • No notifications when the app is closed. Open KRYPT to receive.
  • • Files: up to 100 MB each, one at a time, and only while your friend is online. Keep the app open until 100%.
  • • Chats are 1-to-1 (no groups).
  • • Calls and voice messages need microphone (and camera) permission.
  • • Chats live only on this phone. Clearing browser data, uninstalling, or switching phones deletes them and changes your code. There is no PIN recovery — if you forget it, the only way in is to erase everything.

🔌 If a friend shows Offline

  • • They may not have KRYPT open right now.
  • • Wrong or mistyped code — ask them to share it again.
  • • Some mobile carriers, school/office Wi-Fi and VPNs block direct links. KRYPT has no relay server, so it may not connect on those networks. Try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data, and turn off VPN.
  • • No internet, or the free connection server is busy — wait a minute.

🔒 Privacy

  • • Messages travel directly between phones, encrypted. Nothing is stored on a server.
  • • A friend you're connected to can technically see your network address (IP). Only add people you trust.
  • • Saved chats use AES-256 encryption and need your PIN. A 6-digit PIN can still be guessed by an expert who copies your phone's data, so avoid obvious PINs.
  • • Use ☢️ Nuke to wipe a chat on both phones, or Disappearing messages to auto-delete.
